The alert compares mean pixel brightness in the latest 100 predictions with training data. It needs 20 predictions and a shift above 2 training standard deviations. This is a teaching heuristic, not a formal drift test.

Metrics live in one server process and reset on restart. Accuracy requires labeled outcomes and cannot be measured from these requests alone.
